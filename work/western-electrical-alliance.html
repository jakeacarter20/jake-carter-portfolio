A contractor site that had to speak for every branch.

A six-section marketing site for a Northern Utah electrical contractor working across commercial, multi-family, fire alarm, substation and metering work.

Role
Design and front-end build
Year
2025
Scope
Website, six sections, project gallery
Status
Live

The brief

Western Electrical Alliance has been family-built since 2016 and runs out of three branches in Orem, Logan and Ogden. The problem was that six very different service lines, from fire alarm to power metering, all had to sit on one site without turning the homepage into a directory.

They also needed the site to do a job: get a commercial project manager to a contact form quickly, and give them enough proof on the way there to bother filling it in.

What I did

I structured the site around six sections that follow how a buyer actually reads: who the company is, what it does, where it operates, proof of work, who they would deal with, and how to start a conversation.

The project gallery carries its own pages per job, so a substation build and a student housing complex each get room to be shown properly rather than sharing one grid.

Everything is hand-built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with a slab serif and a working grotesk carrying the industrial weight the brand needed. It is responsive, keyboard navigable and has a skip link.

Outcome

Three branches, six service lines and four gallery case pages sitting inside one site that still opens with a single clear statement.
