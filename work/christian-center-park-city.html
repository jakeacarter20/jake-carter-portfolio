The brief

The Christian Center of Park City runs several programmes that each have their own audience, their own funders and, when I arrived, their own way of presenting themselves. Departments were producing materials independently, which meant donors were meeting several versions of the same organisation.

My remit covers marketing, graphic and UX design, social content and grant writing, which means the same person is responsible for how the organisation looks to a donor, a client and a foundation.

What I did

The annual newsletter is the clearest example of the consolidation. One publication, one cover treatment, the CCPC mark and the org's channels running vertically up the spine, and every department's year reported inside the same system rather than in separate handouts.

Event identity runs off the same foundations. The Summit Exchange material uses the topographic line work and the bird mark to tie a one-off event back to the parent brand without needing a separate logo.
