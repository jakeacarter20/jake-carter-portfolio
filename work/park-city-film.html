A cinema site that had to argue for membership.

A coursework brief on a real subject: Summit County's only nonprofit art house cinema, and a site organised around showtimes, programmes and the case for supporting it.

Role
Web design
Year
2023
Scope
Website
Status
Coursework

The brief

This was a class project rather than a commission, but I took a real cinema as the subject. Park City Film is a nonprofit art house cinema, which means the website has two audiences with different urgencies. Someone checking tonight's showtime wants one thing and wants it in four seconds. A potential donor or member needs the longer argument about why an independent cinema in a ski town is worth funding.

Most cinema sites serve the first audience and forget the second.

What I did

The homepage leads with the mission rather than the listings, because the listings are one tap away in the navigation and the mission is not. 'Creating Community Through Film' sits over cinema interior photography, with the nonprofit status stated immediately underneath rather than buried in an About page.

Below that, three routes: About, Showtimes and Programs, each with its own photography and its own read-more path. That structure lets the casual visitor go straight to showtimes while the interested one keeps scrolling into programming and impact.
