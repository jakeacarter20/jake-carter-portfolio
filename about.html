I design things people actually have to use.

BS in Web Design from Utah Valley University, and four plus years across a product company, the National Park Service, a university marketing team and a nonprofit.

Most of my work sits at the seam between design and marketing. That is a useful place to stand. A brand system that nobody can produce content in is not finished, and a campaign pointing at an interface nobody can navigate is money spent badly.

I started in production design at Qualtry, preparing artwork that had to survive being pressed onto a physical object. Five years of that teaches you constraints before it teaches you taste, which turned out to be the right order. From there I designed phone experiences for the National Park Service on the Timpanogos Cave trail, spent a summer designing recruitment work across all ten departments of Utah Valley University's College of Engineering and Technology, and then moved to the Christian Center of Park City, where I run marketing, design and grant writing across every department.

Alongside that I design and build websites end to end, in Figma and then in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Being the person who both draws it and ships it means fewer things get lost in handoff.
